Our First Day in SSS3

Dorin Deen School of Excellence

Our first day as SSS3 students at Dorin Deen School of Excellence was a memorable and exciting experience. We spent time playing, interacting, and getting to know one another as we began this important stage of our academic journey.

The day was also special because two of our classmates were entrusted with new responsibilities as student prefects.

The young man pictured standing has now been appointed as the prefect responsible for helping to monitor and address issues involving latecomers.

The young woman pictured has been appointed as a prefect responsible for promoting communication and understanding among students from different tribes and language backgrounds.

As part of our commitment to improving our communication skills, we encourage ourselves to speak English only within the school environment. This helps us develop confidence, improve our spoken English, and communicate more effectively with one another.

These responsibilities were entrusted to the students by ASP Yakub Jumma Jorkie, who continues to encourage discipline, leadership, unity, and personal development among students.

Our first day in SSS3 was not just about entering a new class—it was the beginning of a new chapter filled with friendship, responsibility, leadership, learning, and growth.

👨‍⚕️ Moses Baimba Jorkie's Career

Moses Baimba Jorkie first worked as a teacher and later became a doctor. He also served as an Honourable Member representing Constituency 080 for 5 years and Constituency 091 for another 5 years. He lost his political position in 2023.

He is now the Regent Chief of Jong Chiefdom.

He loves his family and the people he serves.

📅 September 5, 1997

Today in Sierra Leone History

On September 5, 1997, Sierra Leone was going through a very difficult period in its history. In Freetown, thousands of people took part in demonstrations during the country's political and military crisis. The day also included funeral activities for people who had lost their lives during the violence.

September 5, 1997 reminds us of the importance of peace, unity, justice and protecting ordinary people during times of conflict. Learning about difficult moments in our history can help us understand Sierra Leone's journey and build a better future.

🇸🇱 Kush Crisis in Sierra Leone: The Fight Continues in 2026

Published September 10, 2026

The fight against Kush continues to be one of the major public-health and security challenges facing Sierra Leone in 2026. The issue has raised serious concerns about young people, families and communities, particularly in Freetown.

Kush is a dangerous and highly addictive drug. Because its contents can vary, its effects can be unpredictable and harmful. The crisis has raised concerns about mental health, physical health, drug-related crime and the future of young people.

🚨 Government and security response

Sierra Leone's security agencies continue efforts to stop the trafficking and distribution of Kush and other illegal drugs. Authorities have also continued pursuing drug-related cases and strengthening the national response to organised crime.

🧠 A public-health and social challenge

The Kush problem is not only a law-enforcement issue. It is also a major health and social challenge. Research and community programmes have highlighted the serious effects associated with Kush use and the importance of prevention, treatment and recovery.

🤝 Communities must be part of the solution

Fighting Kush requires more than arrests and seizures. Families, schools, religious organisations, community leaders, health workers, young people and government institutions all have important roles to play.

Young people need access to education, employment opportunities, sports, counselling, mental-health support and safe community spaces.

🇸🇱 A message to Sierra Leone's young people

Kush can have devastating consequences for individuals, families and communities. Anyone struggling with substance use should be encouraged to seek professional medical, mental-health or rehabilitation support.

📢 SAY NO TO KUSH. PROTECT OUR YOUTH. BUILD A HEALTHIER SIERRA LEONE. 🇸🇱
