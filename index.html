🇸🇱 Today's History

September 5, 2026

On September 5, 1997, Sierra Leone was going through a very difficult period in its history. In Freetown, thousands of people took part in demonstrations during the country's political and military crisis. The day also included funeral activities for people who had lost their lives during the violence.

September 5, 1997 reminds us of the importance of peace, unity, justice and protecting ordinary people during times of conflict. Learning about difficult moments in our history can help us understand Sierra Leone's journey and build a better future.
