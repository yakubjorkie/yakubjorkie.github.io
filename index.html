🏠 Family Name

The Jorkie Family

👨 Father

Moses Baimba Jorkie is the father of the Jorkie family.

👩 His Three Wives

First Wife: Mary Jello Jorkie

Mary Jello Jorkie has 7 children. She lives in Madina Bum, Bonthe District.

Second Wife: Zainab Jabbie Jorkie

Zainab Jabbie Jorkie has 6 children. Sadly, one child died at a very young age. She lives in Hastings, Freetown.

Third Wife: Esther Jorkie

Esther Jorkie has 1 child. She lives in Congo Water, Freetown.

👨‍⚕️ Moses Baimba Jorkie's Career

Moses Baimba Jorkie first worked as a teacher and later became a doctor. He also served as an Honourable Member representing Constituency 080 for 5 years and Constituency 091 for another 5 years. He lost his political position in 2023.

He is now the Regent Chief of Jong Chiefdom.

He loves his family and the people he serves.

🚗 Family Life

Moses Baimba Jorkie has 7 cars.

🌍 Family Background

The Jorkie family are from the Sherbro and Mande communities and live in Bonthe District, Sierra Leone.

🚘 Current Driver

The current driver of Moses Baimba Jorkie is Shaku Koroma, who is popularly known as Papane.

Papane is a Temne man from another district. He is married to Kadiatu, who is from Madina Bum, and they have three children.

📅 September 5, 1997

Today in Sierra Leone History

On September 5, 1997, Sierra Leone was going through a very difficult period in its history. In Freetown, thousands of people took part in demonstrations during the country's political and military crisis. The day also included funeral activities for people who had lost their lives during the violence.

September 5, 1997 reminds us of the importance of peace, unity, justice and protecting ordinary people during times of conflict. Learning about difficult moments in our history can help us understand Sierra Leone's journey and build a better future.
